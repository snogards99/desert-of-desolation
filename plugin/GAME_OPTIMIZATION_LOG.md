# Desert of Desolation v0.4.0 — Ten Optimization Passes

1. **Architecture** — Removed Railway from the active design; Drive is authority, ChatGPT orchestrates, GitHub/Codespaces is optional code/compute.
2. **Hot path** — Eliminated broad Drive searches from normal turn execution when authoritative IDs/manifests exist.
3. **Player agency** — Tightened no-invention rules for Snogard and materially consequential party choices.
4. **Mechanics-before-prose** — Required committed mechanics before consequential narration, SFX, monster attack/death cues, or state mutation.
5. **Turn pacing** — Added a compact eight-step turn loop and reduced unnecessary confirmation for reversible actions.
6. **Combat clarity** — Preserved tactical state while allowing concise cinematic narration and batched obvious resolution.
7. **Exploration/story** — Strengthened discovery gates, purposeful NPC dialogue, environmental continuity, clues, and escalating dread.
8. **Media/preload** — Prioritized current-scene essentials, bounded successors to two safe branches, and required stale cancellation.
9. **Audio experience** — Preserved explicit playback states, 12-voice adaptive budget, speech-first ducking, epoch invalidation, and monster cue gates.
10. **Cross-platform/resume** — Added constrained-device shedding rules and semantic save/resume reconstruction without stale one-shot replay.

Result: lower latency, clearer choices, tighter state correctness, stronger spoiler safety, and a cleaner path to real layered audio once a GitHub-hosted renderer is connected.
