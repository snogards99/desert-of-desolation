# DOD_SITE component contract

Use real components: DodHeader, DodEyeMark, DodHero, DodTitleLockup, DodPrimaryAction, DodSecondaryAction, DodFeatureCard, DodPanel, DodJournalPanel, DodDialoguePanel, DodInventoryPanel, DodAudioControl, DodNavigation, DodFooter, DodLegalNotice.

Keep the eye silhouette stable. Use aged gold for titles/rules/active controls, midnight blue/indigo/violet for dominant surfaces, warm ivory for text, and spectral cyan only for focus/supernatural accents. Avoid glassmorphism, neon gradients, SaaS dashboards, and generic RPG icon grids.

Title/home surfaces use DOD_SITE. Major cinematic art uses DOD_PAINTED. Ordinary room/NPC/object/journal imagery uses DOD_INK. Missing images fall back to text or an approved background without blocking play.
