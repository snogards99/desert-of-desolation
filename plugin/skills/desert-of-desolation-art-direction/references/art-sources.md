# Art sources and provenance

Use `art-sources.json` for file/page references and per-item credit confidence. All source observations are based on the user's supplied images and PDFs. No inaccessible social gallery is claimed reviewed.

The I3 cover is the primary painted reference and I3 PDF page 6 (printed page 5) is the primary ink specimen. The two clipped images in `specimens/` are extracted source artwork for private style review, not new generated work. Original PDFs and supplied images remain unchanged.

The compilation credit page (PDF page 2) visibly credits Keith Parkinson for the cover. I4 PDF page 69 credits Jim Holloway for the cover and Keith Parkinson for the interiors. Do not assign all interiors to one artist. Where a credit is supplied by the user but not established on the inspected scanned credit page, retain that exact qualification in the register.

I5's provided front cover shows a Skysea encounter, not a room within an ancient tomb. This is a description of the picture, not a claim that the encounter has occurred in the game.

B4, X4, and X5 are secondary period references only. They add no campaign canon. The damaged B4 image and worn duplicate I3 image are not used as production sources.

A provided file is not evidence of public-republication rights. Preserve source copyright and existing host legal/footer content; do not manufacture permission under a marketplace agreement. Before live reuse of a scan, record the intended scope and permission evidence. Unverified source specimens remain private authoring-only; the runtime theme defaults to gradients and the approved eye emblem.
