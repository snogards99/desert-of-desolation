# Continuity and spoiler rules

Use current verified game state, not a source title or catalogue membership, to decide what exists in a picture. A source-table monster mention is a candidate, not a revealed occupant.

Keep module and edition, node ID, environment ID, actor design ID, actor instance ID, source reference, lighting, camera, frame/state, and explicit revealed details in the private production record. Do not include private metadata in player alt text or public filenames.

Render only what characters can presently perceive. Do not reveal concealed doors, trap geometry, treasures, magic identity, motives, passwords, or future encounters. For an unidentified being, use an appearance-based label, not its secret canonical identity. Darkness and limited sight constrain the art as well as the prose.

Reuse one design for multiple ordinary guardians while preserving each guardian's HP, position, animation state, and fate independently. A death picture requires a confirmed lethal outcome; missing dead art never falls back to alive art. A missing subject may fall back to the environment and text.

Pair existing phases A+A, B+B, C+C. Never regenerate the room for each actor. Lock the approved master and edit variations rather than reinterpreting camera/architecture. Keep audio position in verified world space, never inferred from decorative pixel coordinates.

Preload current approved media, one likely safe interaction, and at most two explicit safe alternatives. Do not fetch hidden art merely because it is not yet displayed. The server must issue only authorized current-scene handles; a browser boolean is not sufficient authorization.

All preview specimens and component fixtures in this release are design-review material. They are not the active campaign and must not be used to restore a save or infer current party equipment.
